connecting…

Unfiltered search lets two independent accounts of the same window vote together — what was seen and what was said. Narrowing to one question gives that up.

What retrieval is bad at: how many, who most, what overall, which part is unlike the rest. Everything at or below the chosen tier runs, cheapest first, and anything whose inputs have not changed is left alone.

What to process

Either alone, or both onto one shared chunk grid. The options below are the ones your choice actually reads.

Samplers — which frames are worth describing, and why

The vocabulary is the configuration for objects and has no useful default: a mismatched list found 2.4 detections per frame where a matched one found 5.1.

A stride over the decimated stream, not over seconds: a sampler counts the frames it was offered, and how many of those there are per second is per_second’s business. This form leaves that at 1, so here a stride of N is one frame every N seconds. Blank means 1 — every decimated frame — for uniform and overview alike: overview is a different question, not a different cadence.

Chunk grid — which modality decides the boundaries

One grid per run. A policy whose stream is switched off is disabled here rather than refused after the upload.

Audio

Whisper does not know who spoke and pyannote does not know what was said; they are separate passes joined by word midpoints. Without a diarizer every segment keeps speaker: null, which is truthful — labelling everything SPEAKER_00 is not. A speaker grid needs this on.

Where it goes

Read off the out/ directory rather than remembered, so a restarted server still knows everything it produced.
